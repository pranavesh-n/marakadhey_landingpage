Origin & Philosophy

Why Marakadhey?

Marakadhey started with a simple problem: the internet is full of useful things we save with every intention of coming back to them — and then completely forget.

Bookmarks become cluttered. Tabs multiply. Notes get buried. Reminders are disconnected from the webpages that caused us to create them.

Marakadhey brings saving, reminders, priorities, snoozing, and browser-based memory into one simple workflow.
